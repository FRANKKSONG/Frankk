# Esther: life brief

Frankk's own answers (October 2026) about how he lives and works. Every design decision should be checked against this. Update it when the answers change.

## Why Esther exists
- "The Book of Esther" is Frankk's alone: a life control panel and a journal of what he does, works on and finishes.
- Esther is the name of the daughter he hopes to have one day. The record should be readable by her years from now.
- It should be "like Jarvis but more elegant, cute and intriguing", and "very usable, artful and engaging to use".

## The one thing that would stop him using it
If it can't immediately capture what happens on his phone, then sync, journal, contextualize, store, display and interact with it in Esther, with Obsidian as the backend.

## Devices and sync
- iPhone 17 Pro (has an Action Button). On the PC about 3/5 of the day (Windows).
- Vault sync: the community plugin **GSync** (by dmitry). Leave it and its settings unchanged unless he approves a change.
- Today he uses the "To Do List" app (Monelyze) on iPhone for tasks and Apple Notes for ideas. Everything else goes unrecorded ("too lazy").
- He will switch, but **widgets are crucial**. Ideally a dedicated phone app. Obsidian mobile is acceptable only if the phone UX is "immaculate".
- He'll move his iPhone Notes into Obsidian once Esther works.
- Writing a to-do in Obsidian is "too much writing".

## How things go wrong
He forgets that a task exists, sometimes forgets the deadline, and procrastinates.

## A weekday
- About 8:00: wake up and check the phone. Mornings are prep time.
- 9:00–17:00/18:00: **B-YOU** office. Design work (Illustrator). B-YOU tasks live in the company's Notion. Esther may read it, and should show it on weekdays during office hours.
- Alongside the day job:
  - **Seround** work: content marketing business with his partner. Frankk does branding and consulting. sundey reposts go TikTok → Facebook Reel with his own "sundeyreel bot". Instructions come from Sun Sun and Tott.
  - Side projects with Claude (workflow systems like Esther, PC modding).
  - House matters via the family WhatsApp group.
- After work, one of three:
  - **Surftape Studio(s)** for music, often until 22:00–23:00 or later.
  - Exercise until about 20:00–21:00, dinner around 21:00.
  - Rest.
- Evening: laptop work or scrolling, then a call with his girlfriend before sleep (about 23:30).
- Sundays vary: keep working or rest.

## The morning check ("checking the car's systems before driving at 120 mph")
1. Task list.
2. "How fucked I am today" in terms of work: a load gauge.
3. Which task to start first, with a shortcut or automation to begin it.
4. Calendar and its details.
5. One place to quickly enter a task, idea, progress, context or a journal line.

## Journal
- Both: Esther writes the daily log from what happened, and he adds his own lines.
- Follow Scott Jenson's idea of working memory (Akademy 2026, "Are we really going to use the same Desktop UX forever?"). Esther should "understand me".
  - **Spatial:** things stay where he put them.
  - **Associative:** things are linked to people, projects and places.
  - **Episodic:** things can be found by when and in what situation they happened.
